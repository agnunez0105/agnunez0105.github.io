Project Context

This project was a UX case study focused on helping students balance academic work, study time, and campus involvement. The app concept was designed to help students manage classes, clubs, and study blocks in one place.

This was a group project between Arianna Nunez, Cecilia Bilski, and Alexander Clarke.

Problem

Students often struggle to manage time between classes, studying, extracurricular activities, and social life. This can lead to stress, missed planning opportunities, and difficulty deciding what to prioritize.

  • Students face conflicts between academics and campus involvement.
  • Study time is often planned too late or not protected.
  • Clubs and social plans can create pressure when deadlines are close.
  • Students need a clearer way to see time commitments in one place.
Design Goal: Help students plan earlier and balance productivity with campus involvement without adding more stress.

Role

My role included contributing to research, persona development, design planning, and prototype direction.

  • Participated in research and interview planning
  • Helped organize affinity mapping insights
  • Contributed to persona development
  • Helped connect student pain points to app features
  • Collaborated with my team on prototype direction

Process

Research & Affinity Mapping

We analyzed student experiences and used affinity mapping to identify patterns related to stress, workload, time management, and scheduling challenges.

Persona Development

We created a persona named Ella to represent students trying to balance academic responsibilities and social involvement. Ella helped us focus the design on reducing uncertainty and supporting better planning.

Design Ideas

Based on our research, we focused on features that help students see their full schedule, protect study time, and stay connected to campus activities.

  • Calendar integration
  • Daily schedule view
  • Automatic study-time insertion
  • Club selection and confirmation
  • Social schedule sharing

Prototype

The prototype demonstrates onboarding, schedule creation, club selection, and automated study planning before activities.

Outcome

The final concept was a scheduling app that helps students view their time more clearly, add clubs, and automatically plan study time around activities.

  • Combines academic and social scheduling in one place
  • Automatically suggests study time before activities
  • Improves visibility of time conflicts
  • Supports healthier balance between school and campus involvement
