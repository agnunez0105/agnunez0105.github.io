Process

Research & Affinity Mapping

We analyzed student experiences and used affinity mapping to identify patterns related to stress, workload, and scheduling challenges.

Persona Development

We created a persona named Ella to represent students trying to balance academic responsibilities and social involvement.

Design Ideas

  • Calendar integration
  • Daily schedule view
  • Automatic study-time insertion
  • Club selection and confirmation
  • Social schedule sharing

Prototype

The prototype demonstrates onboarding, schedule creation, and automated study planning before activities.
