Project Context

This project was a UX case study focused on helping students balance academic work, study time, and campus involvement.

Problem

Students often struggle to manage time between classes, studying, extracurricular activities, and social life.

Role

I contributed to research, persona development, design planning, and prototype direction.

Process

Our team conducted research, identified student pain points, created personas, and developed a scheduling concept to reduce stress and improve planning.

Outcome

The final concept was a scheduling app that helps students view their time more clearly, add clubs, and automatically plan study time around activities.
